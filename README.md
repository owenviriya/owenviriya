## 🏆 Competition highlights

| Result | Competition | Problem | Approach & outcome |
|:--:|---|---|---|
| 🥇 1st | **ACTION 2026** · UNESA<br>[`action-2026-data-mining`](https://github.com/tukangdata-lomba/action-2026-data-mining) | Route 23,000+ Indonesian social-media complaints to 9 public-service pillars | LoRA fine-tuned Sailor2 LLMs (1B–8B), **macro F1 0.940**; final-round sentiment **0.760 vs 0.491** TF-IDF baseline |
| 🥇 1st | **INCEPTION 2026** · UPN Veteran Jatim<br>[`inception-2026`](https://github.com/tukangdata-lomba/inception-2026) | 5-day volatility forecast of the Jakarta Composite Index | Linear-leaf LightGBM on 38 leakage-tested features, **RMSE −26% vs naive**, −10.5% vs HAR-RV (9-year backtest) |
| 🥇 1st | **JOINTS 2026** · UGM<br>[`joints-x-inspire-2026`](https://github.com/tukangdata-lomba/joints-x-inspire-2026) | Day-4 to day-10 ticket sales for 160 films across 69 cities | Cross-validated **MASE 0.337**, 54% below naive, 10.5% below LightGBM |
| 🥈 2nd | **ISFEST 2026** · UMN<br>[`isfest-2026-datcom`](https://github.com/tukangdata-lomba/isfest-2026-datcom) | 30-minute utilization of 150 EV charging stations | EDA-driven multiplicative structural model that beat LightGBM, **holdout R² 0.954** |
| 🥉 3rd | **Schematics NPC 2026** · ITS<br>[`npc-2026`](https://github.com/tukangdata-lomba/npc-2026) | Open-set bird-call classification (3 species + Unknown) | Perch 2.0 embeddings + rejection rule, **macro F1 0.888** with no Unknown training data |
